## Generated video fixtures

`blank.mov` is generated by `scripts/generate-video-fixture.py`. `blank-h264.mov` and `blank-h264-portrait.mov` are small compressed derivatives for passthrough trimming and orientation tests. All contain only the generated solid-color frame, with no people or audio. FFmpeg is an optional fixture-generation tool; the app and test runner do not invoke it.

```sh
ffmpeg -i GravelFitTests/Fixtures/blank.mov -an -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 28 -preset fast -movflags +faststart GravelFitTests/Fixtures/blank-h264.mov
ffmpeg -display_rotation:v:0 90 -i GravelFitTests/Fixtures/blank-h264.mov -c copy GravelFitTests/Fixtures/blank-h264-portrait.mov
```
